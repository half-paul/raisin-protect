# Access Reviews

Access reviews ensure that user access to systems is appropriate and follows the principle of least privilege.

## How It Works

Access reviews in Raisin Protect follow a structured workflow:

1. **Connect** identity providers (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, etc.)
2. **Sync** users, roles, and access records from your providers
3. **Create** review campaigns scoped to specific resources and criticalities
4. **Launch** campaigns to generate individual review records for each access entry
5. **Review** each access grant — approve, revoke, flag, or delegate
6. **Complete** campaigns and execute revocation decisions

!!! note
    Access reviews are currently available via the API. A full UI is planned for a future release.
