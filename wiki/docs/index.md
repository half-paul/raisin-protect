# Raisin Protect Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for **Raisin Protect** — the AI-native GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) platform for managing compliance, risk, and security operations.

## What is Raisin Protect?

Raisin Protect is a multi-tenant platform supporting SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS v4.0.1, GDPR, and CCPA. It provides:

- **Compliance Management** — Activate frameworks, map controls to requirements, track coverage
- **Evidence Management** — Upload, version, and link evidence artifacts with freshness tracking
- **Continuous Monitoring** — Real-time control health heatmaps, automated test runs, alert management
- **Policy Management** — Full lifecycle from draft to published with approval workflows and templates
- **Risk Management** — Risk register with 5x5 scoring, heat maps, treatment plans, and gap analysis
- **Audit Hub** — Manage audit engagements, PBC requests, findings, and readiness dashboards
- **Access Reviews** — Identity provider integration, review campaigns, and access decisions
